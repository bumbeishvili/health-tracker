## Weight Lifting Strategies

### Pyramid Sets for Compound Exercises
For key compound exercises, implement a pyramid set structure:
- **Set 1**: 17.5kg (warm-up/preparation set with perfect form)
- **Set 2**: 22.5kg (heaviest working set for maximum strength development) 
- **Set 3**: 17.5kg (volume completion set maintaining proper form despite fatigue)

This approach is recommended for:
- DB Bench Press
- DB Shoulder Press
- Single-Arm DB Rows
- Bent-Over DB Rows
- Romanian Deadlifts
- Goblet Squats (using appropriate weights)

For smaller isolation exercises (lateral raises, tricep extensions, etc.), maintain consistent weight across all sets as specified in the workout tables.

### Progression Strategy
As you adapt to the pyramid structure:
1. Weeks 1-3: Master the 17.5→22.5→17.5 pattern
2. Weeks 4-6: Try increasing the first and third sets to 20kg
3. Weeks 7-9: Experiment with adding intensity techniques to the final set (slower tempo, brief pauses)
4. Weeks 10-12: Consider 20→22.5→20 for exercises where you've made significant strength gains

## Progressive Overload Plan (Updated with New Weight Options)

### Weeks 1-3: Form and Consistency
- Focus on perfect technique with your current strength level
- Start with the recommended weights
- Establish workout rhythm and consistency
- Track all sets, reps, and weights used
- For exercises where you feel stronger than the suggested weight, increase by 2.5-5kg

### Weeks 4-6: Increase Weight
- Move up to the next weight tier for all exercises where form is solid
- For bench press, consider adding bands for additional resistance if 22.5kg feels manageable
- For rows and pulls, progress to your 22.5kg dumbbells if appropriate
- Maintain rep ranges (keep quality repetitions)
- Continue tracking progress
- Goal: Increase strength while maintaining form
- **Calorie Effect**: Expect 5-10% increase in calorie burn as weights increase

### Weeks 7-9: Add Volume
- Add 1 set to key compound exercises (presses, rows, squats, deadlifts)
- Move up to the next weight tier where possible (e.g., from 15kg to 17.5kg, or 17.5kg to 22.5kg)
- Focus on mind-muscle connection
- Goal: Increase muscle endurance and hypertrophy
- **Calorie Effect**: Expect 10-15% increase in calorie burn from added volume

### Weeks 10-12: Manipulate Tempo
- Continue using your heavier weight options (17.5kg and 22.5kg dumbbells)
- Introduce slower eccentric (lowering) phases (3-4 second count)
- Add pauses at bottom position on key exercises
- Return to original set scheme but with tempo changes
- Goal: Maximize muscle fiber recruitment and stimulation
- **Calorie Effect**: Tempo manipulation may increase calorie burn by 5-10% due to increased time under tension

## Modifications & Adaptations
- If an exercise causes discomfort, substitute with an alternative that targets the same muscle group
- For time constraints, you can convert workouts to supersets (pairing exercises back-to-back)
  - **Calorie Effect**: Supersets may increase calorie burn by 15-25% due to reduced rest
- For extra intensity, add drop sets to your final set of 1-2 exercises per workout
  - **Calorie Effect**: Drop sets may increase calorie burn by 10-20% for those exercises
